SIGNAGE & WAYFINDING

As one of the leading manufacturer of Signage & Wayfinding signs; we offer a wide range of products that contribute to the safety and enhancement of our urban space

At Microprint, we’re reshaping urban navigation by offering different types of wayfinding signs designed for your needs.

With a 30 years of experience, we understand the evolving urban landscape and adapt our processes to deliver effective, innovative wayfinding signage.

CHANNEL LETTERS

These are 3-dimensional letters, numbers or characters usually 3-6 inches deep made out of aluminum or stainless steel.

Channel Letters are often used as a brand’s primary outdoor sign. The depth of this sign literally makes the brand stand-out!

PRO - Channel Letter signs can be made
front, back or halo lit.

BOX SIGNS

Box signs are usually lightbox signages which are attractive and one of the most effective ways to improve your business presence.

Box Signs have a huge customizable option with respect to size, shape, style or graphic, and signs with printed, painted, or embossed faces.

PRO - Box Signs increases brand visibility not only at night but in the day as well.

PYLON SIGNS

Get your brand noticed from a great distance to passing traffic. With this heavy duty pylon sign installed in your premise be assured of getting greater benefits-

  • • High visibility

  • • Looks Attractive

  • • Easier to be Found

  • • Brand Awareness

PRO - A pylon sign literally makes your brand Stand-Out!

NEON SIGNS

One of the classics that brands still consider modern & futuristic are Neon Signs. The bright glow emitted from a neon sign easily grabs the attention of the passing audience.
Date back all the way to the 1920s, these signs are still popular majorly among the restaurants & bars. May it be a simple ‘Open / Close’ sign or just some artistic wall decor piece to elevate your space. Indoor neon signs have a great impact in darker spaces.

PRO - Neon Signs never run out of style!

LIT SIGNS

LED Lit Signs are extensively used for commercial signage. But now-a-days beyond commerce, LED signs are getting popular among community groups, hospitality, retail, government buildings, residence, institutes, memorials & public parks.
With our amalgamation of a Front, Back or a Halo Lit LED Sign, you can create a visual impact on your brand.

Pro - LED Lit Signs being robust and durable, are pocket-friendly and eco-friendly!

WAYFINDING SIGNS
