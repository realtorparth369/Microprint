Microprint is a unique design & fabrication studio with a strong focus on signage and print media which dramatically impacts the perception of each and every individual with respect to the environment & their surroundings.

Most of our print media applications are reflected on Vehicle Decals, flyers, brochures, posters, banners, vinyl graphics, glass graphics, stationary collectibles, cheque printing, magnet decals & apparels.

With a constant growing demand for branding signages, we are proud to have a dedicated team that thrives to create a visual experience in terms of design, fabrication & installation for channel letters, box signs, lit signs, neon signs, and more...

WHAT WE DO.

Our process is basically a journey that begins with ideation & planning, design exploration, design development, mock ups, fabrication, & lastly final product installation.

We strictly adhere to the brand guidelines set by the company client along with providing a visually appealing product that narrates the brand’s story.
